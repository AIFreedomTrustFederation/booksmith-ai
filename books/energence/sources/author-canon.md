# Author Canon: The Energence Hypothesis

Author: Zechariah Daniel Edwards
Status: accepted author source
Editorial rule: preserve the claims below as the author's theory. Corrections, qualifications, and alternative interpretations belong in clearly marked editorial or scholarly discussion; they must not silently replace the canon.

## Foundational claim

All existence was caused by the synthesis of Energy and Intelligence called God. In this theory, intelligence does not initially mean reflective consciousness. It means the information-bearing and formative capacity through which energy assumes coherent relationships and forms.

The divine source is named **Energence**: the gender-transcendent unity of primordial Energy and Intelligence from which spacetime, matter, life, subconscious integration, consciousness, and transcendence emerge.

## Trinitarian modes

- **Father — Nonconscious:** primordial energy-information, causal possibility, physical states, relations, and constraints before biological consciousness.
- **Holy Spirit-Mother — Subconscious:** the formative, synthesizing, regulating, adaptive, life-preserving activity beneath reflective awareness.
- **Resurrected Christic Consciousness — Conscious:** embodied existence awakened to its origin, unity, responsibility, and capacity for self-transcending communion.

## Evolutionary claim

Evolution is not an alternative to divine creation. Evolution is the immanent mechanism by which Energence manifests natural occurrences, diversifies living form, develops information-processing systems, and brings forth conscious beings capable of understanding the laws of the universe.

## Panentheistic claim

Everything exists within God, including nonconscious quantum information, matter, biological regulation, subconscious synthesis, and conscious experience; yet God exceeds every presently manifested form. Every physical state participates in Energence informationally, but not every physical state is individually conscious.

## Teleological claim

Existence tends toward increasingly comprehensive actualizations of coherence, intelligence, relationship, consciousness, and the highest perfection finite minds can progressively apprehend as Godhood. This is a theological interpretation to be argued carefully rather than assumed to be a settled measurement of evolutionary biology.

## Scriptural thesis

Scripture supplies a grammar of divine participation: in God all beings live and move; in the Logos all things hold together; from, through, and toward God are all things; creation groans toward liberation; and resurrection culminates in God becoming all in all.

## Scientific integrity rule

The book must distinguish established evidence, scientific hypothesis, philosophical inference, scriptural exegesis, and constructive theology. Compatibility with evolution is not presented as empirical proof of every metaphysical claim. The work will state what evidence supports, what Energence interprets, and what remains open.
