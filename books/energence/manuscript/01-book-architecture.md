# Book Architecture

## Part I — Naming the Unnamed

1. **Why Energence?** — The gender-transcendent divine ground and the distinction between Energence and ordinary emergence.
2. **What Kind of Claim Is This?** — Science, abductive metaphysics, Scripture, and constructive theology.
3. **In God We Live** — Acts 17, Romans 11, Colossians 1, and Ephesians 1 as a grammar of participation.
4. **Beyond He and She** — Divine gender symbolism, Wisdom, Fatherhood, and Mother-Spirit.

## Part II — The Three Modes of Energence

5. **Father: The Nonconscious Ground** — Primordial energy-information without particle consciousness.
6. **Holy Spirit-Mother: The Subconscious Womb** — Formation, homeostasis, inheritance, memory, and adaptation.
7. **Son: Energy Informed as Matter** — Logos, embodiment, and matter as divine manifestation.
8. **Resurrected Christic Consciousness** — Consciousness transformed from isolated ego into communion.

## Part III — Evolution as Divine Immanence

9. **Let the Earth Bring Forth** — Genesis and genuine creaturely productivity.
10. **From Quantum Information to Life** — Physical information, chemistry, self-organization, and biology.
11. **Evolution Is Not the Enemy of Creation** — Mutation, selection, drift, symbiosis, and niche construction.
12. **The Evolution of Consciousness** — Regulation, sensation, memory, prediction, reflection, and moral agency.

## Part IV — Evil, Death, and Extinction

13. **The Cost of a Becoming Creation** — Predation, suffering, contingency, and extinction.
14. **I Create Calamity** — Isaiah 45, sovereignty, disaster, and the refusal to call suffering morally good.
15. **Satan, the Accuser, and the Divine Council** — Job, Kings, Zechariah, Psalms, and developing traditions.
16. **Selection Without Social Darwinism** — Cooperation, mutualism, symbiosis, and Christic flourishing.

## Part V — The Omega of Communion

17. **Creation Waiting to Be Liberated** — Romans 8 and ecological participation.
18. **The Last Enemy Is Death** — First Corinthians 15 and transformed embodiment.
19. **God All in All** — Reconciliation, divine fullness, and participation without erasing difference.
20. **The Ethics of Christic Evolution** — Humility, honesty, sobriety, justice, care, and reconciled relationship.

## Editorial protocol for every chapter

Each chapter must contain:

1. A governing question.
2. Primary Scripture read in literary and historical context.
3. Scientific material stated at the appropriate level of consensus.
4. The distinctive Energence interpretation, explicitly identified as interpretation.
5. The strongest objection and a fair response.
6. Ethical consequences and safeguards.
7. A concise synthesis and transition.
8. Source notes, citation candidates, figure opportunities, and unresolved author decisions.
