\chapter{Federation Source Map and Epistemic Boundaries}

This monograph is a sovereign Booksmith project inside a larger AI Freedom Trust Federation library. Its purpose is not to collapse the neighboring books into one undifferentiated doctrine, but to make the relationships among them explicit so that scientific argument, metaphysical interpretation, theological reflection, and engineering infrastructure can remain connected without being confused.

The nearest technical neighbor is \emph{The Fractal-Holographic Quantum Consciousness Model} project, which already carries active Booksmith infrastructure for equations, citations, figures, scientific-integrity appendices, proofing, and publishing. The present work borrows that production architecture but does not inherit its scientific claims automatically. Where the two projects overlap in consciousness, coherence, wave structure, or observation, the current monograph applies its own admissibility--occupancy and descriptive-adequacy rules before any claim is carried across.

\emph{Towards a Unified Reality} was originally registered as a physics-to-metaphysics bridge. The present monograph is the maturation of that seed into an active research book. The bridge is now governed by a stronger sequence: mathematical description first, descriptive adequacy second, empirical sufficiency third, and ontology only after those layers have been separated. This ordering is intended to prevent the metaphysical destination from being smuggled into the mathematical premises.

\emph{Universal Process Theosophy}, \emph{Panentheism}, and \emph{Energence} remain related interpretive and doctrinal works. They contain broader claims about process, divinity, immanence, transcendence, consciousness, and spiritual meaning. Those works may interpret mathematical structures developed here, but they are not evidence for the mathematical or empirical claims in this monograph. Conversely, no theorem about invariant form, wave conjugation, or relational resolution is permitted to become theological proof merely because it resonates with a doctrinal formulation elsewhere in the library.

The same boundary applies to explicitly Christological language. Logos, Christ-consciousness, Spirit, or theological interpretations of Being and Becoming may be developed in their own canonical books, but they remain separate derivational layers. In this monograph, ``Being'' and ``Becoming'' are philosophical translations of the state/form and form/flow distinctions. They do not function as theological premises.

AIFT-Genesis provides a useful federation-level epistemic discipline. The broader ecosystem distinguishes empirical claims, scientific consensus, active research, interpretive statements, philosophical claims, doctrinal claims, engineering implementations, prototypes, and aspirational goals. The present monograph uses a compatible but more compact claim ledger: exact mathematics, model-specific computation, algebraic capability, methodological proposal, empirical hypothesis, philosophical interpretation, and ontology. The purpose is the same in both cases: every statement should carry the epistemic status appropriate to the evidence that supports it.

The Booksmith repository itself supplies the authorship and provenance boundary. Canonical manuscript source lives in the book project; generated exports are reproducible artifacts rather than editorial authorities. Shared Federation bibliographies provide source memory, while book-specific references document the literature required by this work. The $\ell=5$ verification ledger records the exact identities that a future computer-algebra notebook must reproduce rather than inventing a retrospective transcript. The missing sixth-order PDE reduction remains named as missing rather than being silently reconstructed from the algebraic capability theorem.

The cross-book architecture can therefore be summarized conceptually without flattening it. This monograph develops the mathematical and philosophy-of-science bridge. FHQCM explores a more specific consciousness framework. Panentheism, Energence, and Universal Process Theosophy develop metaphysical and theological interpretation. AIFT-Genesis supplies federation-level epistemic and governance structure. Booksmith supplies source sovereignty, provenance, proofing, and publication machinery. Their coherence lies in explicit cross-reference, not in treating every claim in every repository as though it belonged to one evidential category.

This separation is especially important because the language of the project is intentionally ambitious. The work can speak meaningfully of Being, Becoming, relation, form, interiority, and the limits of physical description without converting those terms into hidden evidence. Strong interpretation is permitted because the source map makes clear where interpretation begins. The mathematical claims stand or fall by their derivations, the empirical hypotheses by their tests, and the theological writings by a different mode of argument altogether.
