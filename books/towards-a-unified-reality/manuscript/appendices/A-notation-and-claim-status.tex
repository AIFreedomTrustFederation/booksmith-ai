This appendix freezes the notation used throughout the book so that one symbol does not quietly change ontological role from chapter to chapter. $\mathscr R$ denotes the underlying relational state space used as the framework's broad mathematical carrier, while $r$ denotes one relational state. $X$ is a raw representation space, $G_{\mathrm{gauge}}$ the group of representational redundancies, $Q=X/G_{\mathrm{gauge}}$ the gauge-reduced state space, and $G_{\mathrm{perm}}$ a problem-specific family of transformations treated as irrelevant to the selected form identity. $\bar Q$ denotes the further reduced space. $\Phi$ is the form map and $\mathcal F$ its codomain. The metrics $d_{\bar Q}$ and $d_{\mathcal F}$ measure state and form distance respectively.

The symbols $\mathscr C$ and $\mathscr V$ are reserved for coherence and relational variation. $\mathcal M$ denotes a constraint manifold and $\mathcal A$ an attractor. $\mathrm{Poss}$ denotes an admissible possibility space and $\mathcal K_{\mathrm{act}}$ an actualization kernel. $U_g$ denotes a representation of a group element and $P_G$ the Reynolds projector. $\mathscr C_G$ denotes finite-group symmetry coherence. The symbol $K$ is reserved for complex conjugation in the wave chapters and $D$ for Platonic duality. $V_\ell^{\mathbb R}$ denotes the real spherical-harmonic representation and $\mathcal H_{\mathbb C}$ the complex wave Hilbert space.

In the ontology chapters, $P_{\mathrm{phys}}$ denotes a candidate physical state description, $E_{\mathrm{exp}}$ the experiential state posited by an ontology, and $Z_\varphi$ the empirically inferred phenomenological-evidence latent. \[

More elaborate reciprocal or feedback models may be introduced when evidence requires additional arrows. $M_P$, $M_Z$, and $M_R$ in the empirical model-comparison chapter denote statistical model classes and should not be confused with the ontological-predication notation above.

\section*{Claim classes}

The book distinguishes four kinds of statements that must not acquire evidential weight merely because they share mathematical typography.

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item \textbf{Exact theorem or algebraic result.} A statement proved from the declared mathematical assumptions and conventions.
\item \textbf{Model-specific numerical or computational result.} A result established for the specified equations, parameters, discretization, or numerical procedure, without universal promotion.
\item \textbf{Framework definition or methodological proposal.} A chosen formal vocabulary, diagnostic, or empirical protocol whose usefulness must be evaluated rather than inferred from notation alone.
\item \textbf{Interpretive or ontological proposition.} A philosophical reading that may organize inquiry but is not established by the mathematical formalism that motivates it.
\end{enumerate}

Appendix G deliberately uses these distinctions on the page: Descartes' Circle Theorem is an established mathematical result; the recursive-relational state $\mathcal X$ is a framework definition; claims about whole/part relativity and scale are interpretive propositions. Recursive packing is not presented as evidence that reality is ontologically recursive.

The model-specific results include the observed octahedral $\ell=4$ and icosahedral $\ell=6$ asymptotic branches in the stated nonlinear spherical PDE and parameter regime, the associated approximate transverse Hessian gaps, and the observed sensitivity of relaxation time to quadratic coupling. These are not promoted to universal theorems.

It proves both that four primitive sextic directions exist and that at least one explicit primitive sextic resolves both quartic shape-flat directions. It still does \emph{not} prove the coefficient with which such structure appears in the complete sixth-order center-manifold reduction of the chosen PDE. The PDE-specific restricted curvature

\[
H_{6,\mathrm{shape}}^{\mathrm{PDE}}
\]

remains open.

The A0--A7 ladder, nested physical sufficiency test, partial information decomposition protocol, complexity-controlled prequential comparison, and defeater rules are methodological proposals. They are not empirical results until applied to data.

Physical-first, experiential-first, and relational dual-aspect ontologies are competing hypotheses whose burdens are formalized by $\mathcal M_P$, $\mathcal M_E$, and $\mathcal M_R$. None is presented as a theorem forced by the mathematical work.
