\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Publisher's Note}

This work belongs to the AI Freedom Trust Federation's source-first publishing library. Its purpose is to preserve a complete intellectual trail while keeping the finished argument readable. Mathematical results, model-specific computations, methodological proposals, philosophical interpretations, and open ontological questions are therefore distinguished throughout rather than flattened into one epistemic category. The manuscript treats human-approved source as canon and machine-assisted synthesis as editorial labor carried out under Booksmith's provenance and verification rules.

The work is intentionally ambitious in scope but conservative about what each derivation is allowed to prove. Its mathematical results should be judged as mathematics, its simulations as model-specific computation, its consciousness program as a research design, and its metaphysical conclusions as interpretations that remain answerable to empirical evidence. The governing purpose is not to weaken the language of discovery, but to make clear where the force of the language comes from.
